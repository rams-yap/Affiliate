The 3 Hidden Chemicals Hiding in Your Non-Stick Pan (And How to Swap Them)

Hi friend,

If you’ve ever noticed small scratches or peeling edges on your favorite non-stick skillet, you’re not alone. Most conventional non-stick cookware relies on chemical coatings (PFAS, PTFE, and PFOA) that begin degrading at temperatures above 500°F.

In this issue of The Non-Toxic Kitchen Journal, we break down the 3 core pillars of building a truly non-toxic kitchen—without compromising on cooking performance or aesthetic beauty.

🌿 Quick Non-Toxic Blueprint Check:

  • Thermolon Mineral Ceramic: Uses sand-derived ceramic silica instead of toxic synthetic polymers.
  • Heavy Metal Safety: Look for independent lab tests verifying zero lead or cadmium leaching.
  • Carbon Steel & Enameled Cast Iron: Naturally non-stick alternative that lasts for generations.
