# Pantry & Pan Team Personas & Image Generation Guide

This document standardizes the official team personas, backgrounds, visual characteristics, and image generation prompts for **Pantry & Pan** (`pantryandpan.com`). 

Use these reference profiles and image paths when generating future scene compositions (e.g. testing in a kitchen, writing at a desk, unboxing cookware, or hosting a picnic).

## 1. Mina Kim

### Profile & Role
- **Title**: Co-Founder & Lifestyle Lead
- **Heritage**: Korean-American
- **Age**: Early 30s
- **Focus**: Mindful daily living, clean pantry organization, Korean *well-being* (웰빙) philosophy, tactile hand-feel, and organic color palettes (**Warm Oat**, **Terracotta**, **Sage**).
- **Narrative Motivation**: Inspired by her mother's slow-simmered broths, handcrafted ferments, and visually peaceful kitchen care.

### Visual Features & Styling
- **Facial Features**: Dark warm almond-shaped eyes, subtle eyebrow angle asymmetry, natural cheekbone structure, delicate laugh lines, micro-freckles on nose bridge, visible natural skin pores.
- **Hair**: Sleek black chin-length bob with a realistic parting and soft loose flyaway strands.
- **Fashion**: Minimalist linen blazer / warm oat cashmere sweater, subtle gold stud earrings.
- **Base Color Palette**: Warm Oat (`#F9F6F0`), Soft Charcoal (`#2C3531`).
